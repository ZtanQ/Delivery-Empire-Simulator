# Box & Truck Rules

## Box contents

- One box contains one product only.
- Each box holds up to 5 units.

## Purchase to boxes

Purchased units are split into boxes of 5.

The number of boxes is:

`Units bought ÷ 5`, rounded up.

The last box can contain fewer than 5 units.

Examples:
- 5 Cola = 1 box
- 10 Cola = 2 boxes
- 12 Cola = 3 boxes (5 + 5 + 2)

## Truck limit

The truck carries a maximum of 6 boxes per delivery, using a 2 × 3 grid.

If a purchase needs more than 6 boxes, the terminal does not allow the purchase.

## Shelf capacity

Each shelf holds a maximum of 20 units.

This works with the 5-unit box size because 4 full boxes fill one shelf.

## Full shelf

A box is placed whole or not at all.

If the full box does not fit on the shelf, the player keeps holding it until space is available.
