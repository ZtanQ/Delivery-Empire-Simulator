# Level 1 Orders

## Overview

Level 1 begins with a single product order. The purpose is to introduce the player to the complete order → stock → fulfilment loop without overwhelming the player with multiple simultaneous requests.

## Level 1 Order

### Order 01 — Starter Restock Order

**Requested products:**

| Product | Quantity |
|---|---:|
| Potato Chips | 5 |
| Cola Can | 5 |
| Whole Milk | 5 |

**Total units:** 15

The order intentionally uses products from three different Level 1 categories so the player interacts with multiple shelf categories during the first order.

## Timer

For **10DS**, the Level 1 order timer starts at:

**90 seconds**

The 90-second value should be treated as the starting value unless the design team agrees on a different value.

## 70/30 Product Rule

The recommended rule for Level 1 is to retain the **70/30 product distribution**:

- **70%** of requested products should come from the expected/common Level 1 product pool.
- **30%** can introduce less-common products to add variety.

This keeps early orders predictable enough for onboarding while still giving the player a reason to maintain broader stock.

The rule should be validated through playtesting rather than treated as a permanent balance value.

## Design Goal

The first order should teach the player to:

1. Read an order.
2. Check available shelf stock.
3. Restock missing products when necessary.
4. Fulfil the order within the timer.
5. Understand that different product categories occupy different shelf areas.

Later Level 1 orders can expand product variety and difficulty once this basic loop is established.
