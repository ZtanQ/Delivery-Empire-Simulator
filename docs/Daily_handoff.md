# Daily handoff template

Post this in the team channel at the END of your working day.
Copy the block, fill it in, post it. Four lines. Two minutes.

---

**Role:** SYS / GP / AI / UI / PERF / DES
**Date:**

**Done today**
-

**Not done**
-

**BLOCKED by**
- (write "nothing" if nothing is blocking you)

**PR open**
- link, or "none"

---

## Rules

- Post it even if the day went badly. A bad day reported is a day the lead can fix.
- Write "BLOCKED" in capitals. It is the word the lead searches for first.
- Never leave your day without either a PR or a written reason there is none.
