# Demand Signal Spec

## Chosen Option
**Demand Forecast**

## How it works
The terminal will show which product categories have high, medium, or low demand for the current day.

Example:
- Drinks — High
- Snacks — Medium
- Household — Low

## When the player sees it
The player sees the forecast **before buying products**. They can check the terminal, see what is likely to be needed, and then decide what to stock.

## Why this option
I chose Demand Forecast because it gives the player useful information before they spend money on products. It lets them prepare for upcoming orders instead of finding out about demand after an order is already active.
